04 — About

A computer engineer who
ended up in search.

I trained as a computer engineer in Bangladesh and started designing commercially in 2016 — logos, thumbnails, video edits. Alongside that I spent my early career in IT and operations, supervising systems at Mechanic Chai and running operations at Fifo Tech. Somewhere in there I started building websites for people, and the part I kept coming back to was why some pages get found and identical ones don't.

In 2022 I made that the job and founded Error House. I moved to the UK, kept the studio running, and now work from Cambridge with clients in three time zones. Alongside client work I run The Paid Hour, a UK freelancer tax site — which is where I test everything before I recommend it.

I'd rather be useful than impressive. If something isn't going to work, I'll say it in the first email. More about how I work →
